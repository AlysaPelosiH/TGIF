Seasonal Wisdom

Year-Round Gardening Calendar

Essential tasks and planting windows tailored to seasonal changes.

Seed Starting & Sowing

  • Start tomatoes, peppers, and eggplants indoors 6 weeks before last frost.
  • Directly sow peas, radishes, spinach, and lettuce outdoors early spring.
  • Pot up indoor foliage plants as new growth begins.

Soil Preparation

  • Top-dress garden beds with 2-3 inches of organic compost.
  • Test soil pH and amend with lime or sulfur if needed.
  • Avoid tilling wet soil to prevent compaction.

Maintenance & Care

  • Prune fruit trees and summer-flowering shrubs before buds break.
  • Begin fertilizing indoor plants with diluted balanced fertilizer.
  • Inspect young shoots for early aphid outbreaks.

Watering & Mulching

  • Water deeply early in the morning to prevent evaporation loss.
  • Apply 2 inches of straw or wood mulch to conserve soil moisture.
  • Monitor container plants daily—they dry out quickly in high heat.

Harvesting & Pruning

  • Pinch back basil and herbs to prevent early flowering and seed set.
  • Prune tomato suckers for higher airflow and bigger fruit yields.
  • Harvest beans, cucumbers, and zuchinnis frequently to encourage production.

Fall Sowing & Bulbs

  • Plant spring-flowering bulbs like tulips, daffodils, and alliums.
  • Sow cool-weather crops: kale, garlic, spinach, and winter carrots.
  • Divide overgrown perennials like hostas and daylilies.

Garden Cleanup

  • Collect fallen disease-free leaves for leaf mold or compost.
  • Protect sensitive outdoor potted plants before the first hard freeze.

Indoor Plant Care

  • Reduce watering frequency as plant growth slows significantly.
  • Move houseplants closer to windows to capture limited sunlight.
  • Wipe down dust from leaves to improve photosynthesis efficiency.

Planning & Prep

  • Order heirloom seeds for upcoming spring garden beds.
  • Clean and sterilize seed trays, pots, and hand tools with dilute soap solution.
